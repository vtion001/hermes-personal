Hermes Personal

A locally operated assistant for organizing email, calendars, documents, and everyday work.

Hermes Personal is Vincent John Rodriguez’s personal installation of the open-source Hermes Agent. It connects only to Google accounts explicitly authorized by the operator. It is not a public sign-up service or an official Google product.

Why Google access is requested

  • Gmail: find and summarize email, prepare drafts, and send or organize messages when explicitly requested.
  • Calendar: read schedules and manage events when requested.
  • Drive, Docs, and Sheets: find, read, create, and update files for authorized tasks.
  • Contacts: look up contact details to support personal productivity.

The consent screen lists the actual permissions requested. Google access can be revoked at any time.

How it runs

The assistant runs on the operator’s computer and uses direct Google APIs. Relevant task content may be processed by the configured AI provider. The privacy policy explains data access, storage, sharing, and deletion. Use is subject to the terms of use.
