Privacy policy

This policy covers Hermes Personal, a personal installation operated by Vincent John Rodriguez. It describes this installation, not every deployment of the upstream Hermes Agent project.

Data accessed and purpose

With explicit Google authorization, Hermes Personal may access email content, headers, attachments and mailbox labels; calendar events; Drive files and metadata; document and spreadsheet contents; and contact names and details. Access depends on the permissions granted and the task requested. Data is used to find information, summarize content, prepare replies, organize schedules, and work with files for the operator.

Actions and control

Read operations support requested tasks. Sending email, changing calendar events, or creating and updating files is subject to the operator’s instructions and applicable approval controls. Google authorization does not itself authorize every possible action.

Storage and retention

OAuth credentials and tokens are stored in permission-restricted files on the operator’s computer. The application does not receive or store Google account passwords. Task results, downloaded files, conversation history and logs may also be retained locally until the operator deletes them. Backups may retain copies until removed. Relevant task content sent to an AI provider is subject to that provider’s applicable account terms, retention settings and privacy policy.

Data sharing and AI processing

Google APIs provide the requested data directly to the local application. Relevant excerpts and task results may be sent to the configured OpenAI/Codex service for inference and returned in the assistant’s responses. The operator should only request processing of information they are authorized to share with that service. This installation does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or use it to develop or train generalized AI models. Data is not published on this website. It is disclosed to additional recipients only when explicitly requested by the operator, or where legally required.

Hermes Personal’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Revocation and deletion

You can revoke access through Google Account connections. Revocation stops future authorized access but does not automatically erase previously saved files, local history, backups, or provider-held records. The operator can remove local tokens and retained data from the computer and its backups. For deletion requests or questions, contact vjrodriguez1994@gmail.com.

This website

This is a static information website hosted by GitHub Pages. It does not include an application login, forms, analytics scripts, or advertising trackers. GitHub may process standard technical request data under its privacy statement.

Changes

This policy may be updated if the installation’s data practices change. The effective date above identifies the current version.
